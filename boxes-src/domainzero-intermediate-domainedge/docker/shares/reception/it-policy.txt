DOMAINZERO -- Password Policy (draft, never finalized)
========================================================
Minimum length: 8 characters
Complexity: required

Policy flag: FLAG3_PLACEHOLDER

Known gap (flagged by IT, never fixed): the default password new
accounts are provisioned with follows the pattern
"<Season><Year>!" (e.g. "Winter2026!") and a lot of people never
change it. No account lockout policy is configured either, so this
is worth testing broadly rather than guessing at just one account.
